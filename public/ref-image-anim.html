Reference — image reveals

Five ways to bring a photograph in.

Each study runs once when it scrolls into view. Use the replay button to watch it again. Everything here is plain CSS keyframes — no library — so any of these can drop straight into a section component.

01

Shutter slices

Eight columns fall in sequence. Each column holds a full-width copy of the photo pushed sideways by left: calc(var(--i) * -100%), so the picture reads as one image even though it arrives in pieces.

02

Aperture

A circle() clip-path opens from the centre while the photo eases back from 145% and loses a blown-out highlight. Two curves of different lengths, so the settle outlives the opening.

03

Developing

A wipe uncovers the frame, then the image resolves out of blur(26px) saturate(0.25) brightness(1.45) — a print coming up in the tray. The filter starts a beat after the wipe so you catch it mid-development.

04

Mosaic

Twenty-eight tiles, each carrying its own crop, popping in on a diagonal wave. The delay is (x + y) * 85ms, which sweeps the assembly from the top-left corner rather than firing at random.

05

Drift

A slanted polygon() edge sweeps left to right while the photo travels the opposite way inside it. Frame and image disagreeing is what gives it the cinematic push.
